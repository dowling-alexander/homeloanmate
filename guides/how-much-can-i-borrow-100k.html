How Much Can I Borrow on a $100k Salary in Australia?

Updated 2 September 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: a $100,000 salary is only one part of a lending assessment. A useful borrowing estimate needs your household costs, dependants, card limits, other debts and loan assumptions. A generic range cannot show whether a particular lender will approve you.

Why two people on $100k can get different results

  • One applicant may have a car loan or unused card limits while another has none.
  • Childcare, rent, insurance and other household costs can change the available repayment surplus.
  • Some income types are shaded or need a history before a lender will use them in full.
  • The lender's assessment rate and policy may differ from the advertised home-loan rate.

Build a useful estimate

Enter your base income first, then add regular commitments and realistic living expenses. Test a second, more cautious version if your circumstances could change. BorrowPower applies a 3 percentage-point serviceability buffer for scenario planning, consistent with APRA's current minimum standard for ADIs, but it cannot replicate every lender policy.

Useful comparison: run your current position, then rerun it with a future repayment such as car finance or higher childcare costs. The difference tells you more than a generic salary range.

What to check alongside borrowing power

  • Use the deposit planner to keep duty, LMI and purchase costs separate from your deposit.
  • Use the repayment estimator at more than one interest rate.
  • Confirm whether a lender will accept all of your income and the loan type you want.
  • Get personal financial or credit advice if you need a recommendation.
