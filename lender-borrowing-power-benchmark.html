Borrowing Power Calculator Comparison

Borrowing estimates are not interchangeable. This page gives you a consistent set of Australian household scenarios to run through BorrowPower and the public calculators from ANZ, CommBank, NAB and Westpac.

Current edition: 2 September 2026. Next review planned for October 2026.

What this comparison is for

It is a transparent planning reference, not a lender ranking or a loan recommendation. Each lender sets its own assessment rate, expense model, credit policy and product assumptions, and those can change without notice. A public calculator result is an indication only; it is not conditional approval.

BorrowPower applies its published Australian tax and serviceability assumptions. The lender columns below link directly to the relevant public calculator so the same profile can be checked at the source. This avoids presenting stale or invented lender figures as current facts.

Monthly comparison method

  1. Use 10 repeatable scenarios Every profile below uses owner-occupied, principal-and-interest repayments over 30 years unless a source calculator applies a different built-in assumption.
  2. Keep the inputs aligned Use the listed income, dependants, living expenses, other monthly debt payments and card limits. Deposit is shown as purchase context because public borrowing tools do not all use it in their borrowing result.
  3. Record the public estimate and source context At each monthly review, record the displayed result, calculator date and any stated product/rate assumption. Note an unavailable field rather than forcing an unmatched input.

Use a result in context

Borrowing capacity is only one part of a purchase decision. Keep your deposit separate from duty and buying costs, test repayments at a range of rates, and ask a lender or licensed professional to assess your personal circumstances before relying on any estimate.
