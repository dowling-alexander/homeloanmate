What upfront costs can apply?

Your cash plan can include a deposit, transfer duty, lender's mortgage insurance (LMI), registration costs, conveyancing, inspections and moving costs. This planner estimates the deposit, duty and LMI using the details you enter. Add a quote or realistic allowance for costs that are personal or transaction-specific.

Why does deposit size matter?

A smaller deposit usually means a larger loan and higher loan-to-value ratio (LVR). Above 80% LVR, LMI may apply. The comparison table helps show the cash, loan and LMI trade-off without assuming one deposit size is right for everyone.
