Calculation assumptions and data sources
  • Tax uses 2026/27 Australian resident rates. Medicare levy is optional and estimated at 2% when enabled.
  • Borrowing power subtracts expenses, debts, HELP/HECS repayment, and 3% of credit card limits, then applies a 3% rate buffer.
  • Stamp duty uses state/territory rates with buyer-type estimates where the available inputs support them. Conditional schemes are noted in the result message.
  • LMI is an approximate owner-occupied full-doc estimate. Actual lender and insurer premiums vary.

What is affecting your borrowing power?

Income is only one part of the estimate. Living expenses, credit-card limits, personal or car loan repayments, HELP/HECS, dependants, the interest rate and the lender-style assessment buffer can all change the result.
